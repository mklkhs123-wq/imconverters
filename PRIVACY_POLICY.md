# Privacy Policy — IMConverter

**Last updated:** August 2026

Menutechnology ("we", "us") operates the IMConverter Android application (the "App"). This page explains how the App handles your information.

## Summary

**IMConverter works 100% offline. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data, photos, or files.**

## Information We Collect

We do not collect any information. Specifically:

- **No account or sign-up** is required to use the App.
- **No photos, images, or PDF files** you create or edit in the App are ever uploaded, transmitted, or sent to us or to any third party.
- **No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs** are included in the App.
- **No usage data, device identifiers, or crash reports** are collected or transmitted.

All image conversion, compression, resizing, cropping, and PDF creation happens **entirely on your device**. Your files never leave your phone.

## Permissions the App Uses

| Permission | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| Camera | Only used if you choose to take a photo directly within the App. |
| Photos / Media (Storage) | Used to let you select existing photos to convert, and to save the converted files back to your device. |
| Notifications | Used only to notify you when a file has finished saving, so you can tap to open it. |

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data — they are used solely to perform the on-device actions you request.

## Third-Party Libraries

The App uses an open-source library (PDFBox-Android) to create password-protected PDF files. This library runs entirely on your device and does not transmit any data over the internet.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from any user.
